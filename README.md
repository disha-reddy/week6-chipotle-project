# Chipotle Case: Digital Transformation Roadmap & QBR

A single-page website that presents an eight-quarter digital transformation plan for Chipotle, a Q7 quarterly business review (QBR) dashboard, and the governance RACI behind it.

## Files

| File | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `Chipotle Case.html` | The complete website. Self-contained: works offline, no install needed. |

The Roadmap & RACI PDF is embedded inside the HTML file and downloads from the site.

## How to open

1. Download `Chipotle Case.html`.
2. Double-click it, or drag it into any modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, Firefox).

No internet connection, server or setup is required.

## Site structure

**Home**
- Intro and the case in brief (8 initiatives, 3 phases, 7 recurring decisions)
- Buttons to open the QBR Dashboard and the Roadmap & RACI
- 01 / The order journey: click each stop (take the order, make the food, hand it over)
- 02 / Decision rights: pick a decision, choose who should be accountable, compare with the plan
- 03 / Team: roles and bios

**QBR Dashboard**
- Overall status and the headline decision for Q7
- Crawl, Walk and Run panels with eight measures, targets, status and owners
- Decisions required this quarter

**Roadmap & RACI**
- 00 Phases · 01 Roadmap timeline and dependencies · 02 Scope and checkpoints
- 03 Why this order and launch gates · 04 Governance RACI · 05 Accountability · 06 Evidence
- Download PDF button

Use the top navigation or the "Back to home" link to move between pages.

## Data and sources

- The roadmap draws on Chipotle's 2018–2023 annual reports and 2024 proxy statement (sources S1–S6 on the Evidence section).
- The sequence, pilot sizes, targets, budget and decision owners are the team's proposal.
- Q1–Q8 are project quarters, not calendar dates.
- All Q7 dashboard figures are illustrative and are not company results.
