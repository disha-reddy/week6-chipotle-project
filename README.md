# Chipotle Case: Digital Transformation Roadmap & QBR

A single-page website that presents an eight-quarter digital transformation plan for Chipotle, a Q7 quarterly business review (QBR) dashboard, and the governance RACI behind it.

## How to open

1. Download `index.html`.
2. Double-click it, or drag it into any modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, Firefox).

No internet connection, server or setup is required.

## Data and sources

- The roadmap draws on Chipotle's 2018–2023 annual reports and 2024 proxy statement (sources S1–S6 on the Evidence section).
- The sequence, pilot sizes, targets, budget and decision owners are the team's proposal.
- Q1–Q8 are project quarters, not calendar dates.
- All Q7 dashboard figures are illustrative and are not company results.
